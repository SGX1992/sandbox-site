This is the program

SANDBOX is the landmark startup investment program from Oraseya Capital, designed for ambitious pre-Seed to Seed stage technology entrepreneurs. If you’re looking to take your startup to the next level, read on…

SANDBOX lays emphasis on exposure, investor-readiness, and growth. Spanning 5 intense months, founders can expect unparalleled access to VCs, guidance from seasoned entrepreneurs, and hundreds of hours of hands-on support from some of the region’s best mentors.

Oraseya Capital, the venture capital fund of DIEZ, was recognised by MAGNiTT as the UAE’s most active investor in 2024 and 2025, and named Venture Capital Firm of the Year by Entrepreneur Middle East.

“Fast become one of the most sought-after startup programs in the region.”

Everything is designed to help you scale and ultimately raise funds. The program is structured around 9 pillars to cover all bases:

  • Product Development
  • Investor Relations
  • Traction
  • Scaling
  • Financial Diagnostics
  • Legal Support
  • Commercial Viability
  • Growth Marketing
  • and uniquely, Wellness

We’re the first startup program in the region to lay emphasis on wellbeing and performance.

What differentiates SANDBOX?

A startupprogram likeno other.

Throughout SANDBOX, founders can expect a flexible curriculum of content and a series of sprints focusing on growth and fundraising. The program sees founders develop a habit of feedback and accountability; rewarding those that make significant progress. SANDBOX is uniquely structured into 2 phases.

01Application process

Three stages. One shot.

3-stage application process including submitting an application form, presenting on a screening call, then pitching to the SANDBOX Selection Committee.

Form → Screening call → Selection Committee
02Phase 1 — 2 months

“Diagnostics”

Deep-dive 2-months focusing on diagnostics, product-market-fit, and positioning.

Diagnostics · PMF · Positioning
03Internal pitch

Pitch. Get funded.

All startups pitch to the SANDBOX Investment Committee. Those shortlisted are invited to Phase 2 and receive an investment of $150,000 from Oraseya Capital via SAFE Note Agreement ($110,000 cash + $40,000 program fee).

$150,000 SAFE
04Phase 2 — 3 months

“Sprints”

Intensive 3-months focusing on investor-readiness, commercialisation, and growth.

Investor-readiness · Commercialisation · Growth
05Finale

SANDBOX Demo Day

At the end of Phase 2, the program wraps up with the SANDBOX Demo Day whereby founders pitch to investors from around the region and around the world.

Regional + international investors
Inside the program
