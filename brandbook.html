01
Strategy

This is the program.

SANDBOX is not a course, a coworking deal or a pitch competition. It is the landmark startup investment program of a venture fund. Every brand decision starts from that fact: the fund invests, the founders work, the result is a company that can raise.

Positioning

Dubai's only VC-backed startup investment program.

Taken verbatim from the website hero. It is the one claim every other message has to support. Never soften it to "accelerator" or "bootcamp" in headline positions.

The brand idea

Cross the line. Then multiply.

The slashed X in the wordmark carries the idea: a founder crosses from idea to investable company, and the X is also the multiplier. The slash cuts through the box. Use the X alone wherever the brand needs a gesture and the full logo would be too much.

Audience

Ambitious pre-Seed to Seed stage technology entrepreneurs who want to raise, in the UAE and the wider region. They are busy, sceptical of programs and allergic to fluff. They respond to proof: money, time, structure, names.

They want to hear

  • How much, how long, what happens next.
  • Who invests and who mentors.
  • What previous founders say.
  • What the deadline is.

They do not want

  • "Ecosystem", "journey", "unlock".
  • Vague community promises.
  • Decorative startup imagery.
  • A program that talks about itself.

How to use it

  • Always as Oraseya Capital’s achievement: “Backed by the UAE’s #1 most active investor.”
  • Name the source and the years: MAGNiTT 2024 & 2025.
  • One mention per surface, as a stat tile or a proof line under the Oraseya endorsement.
  • Pair it with the $150,000: the ranking explains why the cheque is credible.

Never

  • “SANDBOX is the #1 accelerator.” The ranking is the fund’s, not the program’s.
  • Without the source. An unsourced #1 reads as marketing.
  • As the headline. The claim stays “Dubai’s only VC-backed startup investment program”; the ranking is the proof under it.
  • In the gradient or on orange. It is typeset in white like every other number.
Brand values, derived from the nine pillars and the founder testimonials
Founder centricity

"SANDBOX is about you: our founders." The program adapts to the startup, not the other way round.

Proof over promise

Numbers, names, cohorts and quotes. If it cannot be shown, it is not said.

Intensity

Five epic months of hard work. The brand is bold, dense and fast, never cosy.

Investor readiness

Everything points to the raise: pitch reviews, datarooms, Demo Day.

Wellbeing

Wellness+ is a pillar, not a perk. Performance and mental health belong together.

02
Voice

Bold. Direct. Founder-first.

SANDBOX speaks like an investor who has already decided to back you and now expects you to work. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers before adjectives.

Do

  • Lead with the number: "$150,000 via SAFE Note Agreement."
  • Address the founder as "you". Call the program "SANDBOX", all caps, in running text.
  • Use imperative CTAs: Apply now. Start your application.
  • Quote founders by name, role and company.
  • Say "5 epic months" exactly like that; it is a brand phrase.

Don't

  • Exclamation marks, except inside a founder quote.
  • "Empower", "unlock", "journey", "ecosystem", "synergy".
  • Write "Sandbox" or "sandbox" in headlines.
  • Promise investment to everyone; only shortlisted startups receive it.
  • Explain Dubai. The audience knows where it is.
On brand

Three stages. One shot. Submit the form, present on a screening call, pitch to the Selection Committee. Applications close end of November.

Off brand

We're thrilled to invite passionate founders on an incredible journey to unlock their full potential in Dubai's thriving startup ecosystem!

Headline pattern

Statement. Payoff. "Gain a winning edge." "A startup program like no other." "Pitch. Get funded." Two beats, the second one shorter.

Vocabulary

Say

  • investment program
  • founders
  • investor-ready
  • cohort
  • Demo Day
  • sprints
  • diagnostics
  • pillars
  • SAFE Note
  • traction

Avoid

  • incubator
  • bootcamp
  • entrepreneurial journey
  • game-changing
  • world-class (as filler)
  • disrupt
  • rockstar
  • hustle
03
Logo

Mark, wordmark, endorsement.

The logo is the wordmark with the Oraseya endorsement. The slash through the X is the only element that may break the geometry; everything else stays locked. Two weights are under review, switch them with the control at the bottom left.

WordmarkOraseya endorsementX treatment
Wordmark

SANDBOX, wide-tracked, drawn from Plus Jakarta Sans. The X is broken into two strokes and the slash completes it, overshooting the cap height. The wordmark is a drawn asset, never typed.

Endorsement

ORASEYA CAPITAL with the compass O, right-aligned under the wordmark at roughly 40% of its height. Drop it only when Oraseya is already the sender, for example inside the fund's own deck.

Three directions under review

A, bold: the medium-weight, wide-tracked cut. B, thin: the light cut the team circled in the August review. C, SB compact: Direction 3 from Figma, tight heavy wordmark led by the SB mark, with the mark doubling as a soft editorial watermark. One becomes the logo; the others are retired.

Clear space

Keep a margin of one S-height on all sides. Nothing enters it: not type, not the X decoration, not a photo edge.

Minimum size
Wordmark 120 px / 30 mm
With endorsement 160 px / 40 mm
SB mark 16 px / 4 mm · edge cases

Below these sizes drop the endorsement first. Only where the wordmark cannot fit (favicon, app icon, avatar) does the SB mark step in.

The X treatment

The slashed X is the brand's gesture. It appears alone as a watermark (at 6–12% opacity), as a section decoration and as a motion element. It never replaces the mark as a logo and is never filled with the gradient.

SB mark · edge cases only
Favicon
Avatar
Print, black

The fused S and B exists for the three places where a wordmark cannot fit: favicon, app or social avatar, and a stitched or stamped mark on swag. It is always flat white on black, or flat black on paper. The gradient version from the explorations is not used.

Rules

Do

  • Place the full lockup on black, white or the blur texture.
  • Use the white version on photography and gradient.
  • Let the slash overshoot the cap height; that overshoot is part of the drawing.
  • Use the X decoration, not the SB mark, when a surface needs a gesture.

Don't

  • Type the wordmark in any font, including Plus Jakarta Sans.
  • Rotate, outline or add shadows to the wordmark.
  • Lead with the SB mark. It is an edge-case asset, never the logo, and never in orange.
  • Place the lockup over the busy middle of a photo; use the dark edge.

05
Texture

The blur is the brand's only image.

SANDBOX does not use stock photos of founders at whiteboards. Its one visual material is motion-blurred orange light: heat, speed and a city at dusk in a single abstraction. On screen it moves; in print it is a still.

Hero · final CTA · loaders

in print

Rules

Do

  • Always lay a black gradient over the bottom third before placing type.
  • Keep the streaks diagonal, bottom-left to top-right.
  • Use it as a full-bleed field or inside a rounded badge, nothing in between.
  • Add 10–15% film grain on large surfaces.

Don't

  • Put body copy directly on it.
  • Tint it purple, blue or pink. The explorations in Figma are closed.
  • Use it behind the paper sections; those stay flat.
  • Stretch it; crop it.

07
Layout

Black stage, paper interludes.

Pages are long, dark and generous. Every third or fourth section flips to paper to reset the eye, then the page returns to black. Content sits on a 1680 px max-width grid with gutters that scale from 20 to 72 px.

08
Motion

Everything arrives.

Nothing on a SANDBOX surface simply appears. Type rises out of a mask, numbers count, the wordmark wipes in and the slash lands last. Motion is fast, heavy and resolved: long expo ease-outs, no bounces, no loops except marquees.

Do

  • One orchestrated entrance per view, then calm.
  • Scroll-bound effects move with the reader and stop when they stop.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion: show everything at rest.
  • Keep marquees slow (0.5–1.2 px per frame) and pausable.

Don't

  • Bounce, wobble or spring, except the release of a magnetic button.
  • Fade in from opacity 0 without a direction.
  • Animate colour; animate position, clip and scale.
  • Autoplay sound, ever.
